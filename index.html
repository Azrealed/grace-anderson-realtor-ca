Meet your agent

Grace Anderson

Grace got her start the hands-on way — working in her family's real estate business, renovating distressed properties and learning what makes a house a home. Somewhere between the hammers and the nails, she realized the part she loved most was the people.

She earned her license in 2020 and hasn't slowed down since, helping dozens of clients buy and sell houses, commercial spaces, vacant land, and even apartment complexes across the North State.

What clients count on most: constant communication, a level head when the unexpected happens, and a genuinely deep knowledge of the Shasta County market.

She practices with Terence Davis & Associates, a boutique Redding brokerage with deep roots in the North State. The team helps local families and investors buy and sell across Shasta County — homes, land, investment, and commercial property — with the kind of personal, transparent service the big franchises can't match.

Where Grace works

Rooted in the North State.

From the slopes of Mount Shasta to the banks of the Sacramento River, Grace helps buyers and sellers across Shasta County and the surrounding North State communities.

Shasta County is one of Northern California's most beautiful places to call home — Mount Shasta to the north, Whiskeytown and Shasta Lake to the west, and the Sacramento River running right through Redding. It's a market Grace knows street by street.

Whether you're after a riverfront home in Redding, acreage in Palo Cedro, a first place in Anderson, or a lake retreat near Shasta Lake, you'll have a local expert who understands each community's character, pricing, and pace.
